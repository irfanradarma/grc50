## Evidence and limits

### HBS / BCG field experiment - 2023

https://aiinstitute.hbs.edu/navigating-the-jagged-technological-frontier/

758 BCG consultants; knowledge-work tasks using GPT-4. Benefits refer to tasks inside the tested capability frontier, not audit accuracy.

### The IIA - AI Auditing Framework - 2024

https://www.theiia.org/globalassets/site/content/tools/professional/aiframework-sept-2024-update.pdf

Professional guidance. The audit canvas and examples in this deck are teaching syntheses, not a reproduction of the official framework.

### NIST - AI RMF 1.0 - 2023

https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf

Voluntary framework: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. Use the 1.0 edition explicitly; it is under revision.

### NIST - Generative AI Profile - 2024

https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf

Cross-sector guidance on generative AI risks; not proof that a particular product is safe.

### NC State / AICPA - State of Risk Oversight - 2026

https://erm.ncsu.edu/resource-center/2026-state-of-risk-oversight-enterprise-risk-management-trends-and-key-findings/

Executive survey: 69% report increased risk volume/complexity over five years; 30% describe oversight as mature/robust. These are different questions, not complementary percentages.

### RSM · Middle Market AI Survey · 2026

https://www.rsmus.com/insights/services/digital-transformation/rsm-middle-market-ai-survey.html

U.S./Canada study: 1,030 respondents from organisations already using AI, including pilots. The 86% integration and 36% fully-embedded figures describe this sample; they do not measure adoption across all businesses or in Indonesia.

### RSM · Survey methodology · 2026

https://www.rsmus.com/newsroom/2026/rsm-survey-middle-market-embraced-ai-now-comes-hard-part

Fieldwork 5–16 March 2026: 827 U.S. and 203 Canadian respondents, management level or above with influence over technology investment. Government and nonprofits excluded. All participating organisations already used AI.

### The IIA · Global Internal Audit Standards · 2024

https://www.theiia.org/globalassets/site/standards/globalinternalauditstandards_2024january9.pdf

2024 edition, effective January 2025. References include Standards 4.3, 11.5, 13.2, 14.1–14.4 and 15.2. The deck paraphrases selected implications for internal audit; it does not replace the Standards or prescribe financial-statement audit requirements.

### ISO/IEC 42001 · AI management systems · 2023

https://www.iso.org/standard/42001

Official ISO overview of the AI management system standard. Organisational governance requirements are distinct from proof of a model’s accuracy. No certification or clause-by-clause conformance conclusion is made in this seminar.

### COSO · ERM: Strategy & Performance · 2017

https://www.coso.org/enterprise-risk-management

COSO connects enterprise risk management with strategy setting and performance. The feedback diagram is an RSM seminar explanation of that connection, not a reproduction of COSO’s official framework or an endorsement of a scoring method.

### Anthropic · Building effective agents · 2024

https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents

Engineering guidance distinguishing predefined workflows from model-directed tool use. Used for the architectural distinction, not a benchmark, audit standard or claim that autonomy is always preferable.

### PCAOB staff · GenAI outreach · 2024

https://pcaobus.org/documents/generative-ai-spotlight.pdf

July 2024 staff observations from limited U.S. audit-firm and preparer outreach, especially pp. 4–5. Historical practice evidence, not a Board rule, a current adoption survey, or Indonesian audit requirements.

### RSM · The responsible auditor

https://www.rsm.global/singapore/responsible-auditor-governing-ai-delivering-trust

RSM regional practitioner discussion with Indonesian participation: AI governance, accountability, human oversight and audit capability. Thought leadership, not a quantitative study.

### NCSC · Secure AI development

https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/guidelines-secure-ai-system-development

Secure design, development, deployment and operation guidance. Supports threat modelling, access, monitoring and lifecycle controls; it does not guarantee confidentiality or certify the reference architecture.

### OWASP · Sensitive data disclosure · 2025

https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm022025-sensitive-information-disclosure/

LLM02:2025. Data sanitisation, least privilege, source restriction and disclosure risk. A control in a prompt or a redaction step is not a complete privacy guarantee.

### OWASP · Prompt injection · 2025

https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/

LLM01:2025. Untrusted content can redirect an LLM application; controls must address the application, tools and permissions rather than relying on a system prompt alone.

### OpenAI · Agent approvals & security

https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-approvals-security

Product-specific guidance on agent permissions, sandboxing and command networking. Shell restrictions do not establish offline model inference or cover every hosted search, app or MCP path. Diagrams simplify these distinctions for general audiences; they do not reproduce the exact interface.

### OpenAI · ChatGPT Work enterprise overview

https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/chatgpt-work-overview

Enterprise/Edu Work documentation: conversations, uploaded files, memory and connected sources can have distinct retention and deletion lifecycles. Not a universal consumer ChatGPT or API policy.

### Anthropic · Claude Code data usage

https://code.claude.com/docs/en/data-usage

Claude Code documentation distinguishes consumer and commercial data use, model-improvement choices and feedback/session-sharing. Conditions depend on the account and feature; not a statement of every provider’s terms.

### Anthropic · Claude Code permissions

https://code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions

Supported tool permissions, file-read restrictions and scopes. Read-only, working directory and gitignore are not interchangeable privacy boundaries; verify the actual tool configuration.

### Anthropic · Claude Code user FAQ

https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14554922-claude-code-user-faq

Primary product explanation: CLI tools can read local source content and send it to hosted inference. This is specific to cloud-backed Claude Code use, not every local agent or offline model.

### NIST · Trustworthy AI characteristics

https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/3-sec-characteristics/

AI RMF 1.0 section 3 and Figure 4. Seven interconnected qualities, with validity/reliability as a foundation and accountability/transparency across the others. Context and trade-offs matter; this is not an audit certification checklist.

### OpenAI · API data controls

https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data

API-specific documentation reviewed 13 November 2026: API data is not used for training by default unless opted in. Retention and processing controls are separate. Do not apply this statement to every personal chatbot account or feature.

### Microsoft MSRC — CVE-2025-32711

https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2025-32711

Vendor advisory for a resolved M365 Copilot information-disclosure vulnerability. This case establishes a real AI attack surface, not evidence of a confirmed customer breach.

### RSM Indonesia — Internal Audit

https://www.rsm.global/indonesia/sites/default/files/media/services/Internal%20Audit.pdf

Public RSM Indonesia internal-audit service information. Specific AI penetration-testing and AI-supported assessment capabilities are supplied by the RSM presenters, not independently demonstrated by this brochure.

### NVIDIA — Self-hosted RAG Blueprint

https://docs.nvidia.com/rag/latest/deploy-docker-self-hosted.html

Technical deployment reference for self-hosted, on-premises RAG components. The location diagram is a teaching architecture, not a security certification or a claim that RAG prevents retention or training.

### Microsoft Research — Natural-language data analytics

https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/conversational-data-analytics/

Research into turning natural-language questions into analysis programs. Supports a changed interaction model, not a claim that LLMs eliminate statistical testing or are accurate on every analytical task.

### scikit-learn — Cross-validation and model evaluation

https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html

Documentation on evaluating models with held-out data and cross-validation. Supports the distinction between exploratory LLM assistance and validated predictive modelling.

RSM-specific AI pentesting, internal-audit process reengineering, on-premises AI implementation and COBIT assessment capabilities are supplied by the presenters. No undisclosed client, outcome or success rate is implied.

RAG supplies selected context at inference time; it does not mean company text is never read by a model. The conservative location design keeps every processing component local. LLM-first ad-hoc exploration differs from a validated predictive modelling project.
